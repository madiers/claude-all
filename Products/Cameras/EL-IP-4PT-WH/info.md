## Description
### A 4-megapixel PTZ camera with optical zoom and intelligent auto-tracking for active coverage.

The Nice EL-IP-4PT-WH is the pan-tilt-zoom member of the updated Nice surveillance camera line. With a motorized varifocal lens, 5x optical zoom, and person & vehicle auto-tracking, it actively follows targets across the scene while delivering color night vision and active deterrents.

- 4MP resolution with 2.7mm-13.5mm motorized lens and 5x optical zoom
- Person & vehicle auto-tracking keeps targets in frame
- Color night vision powered by advanced AI-ISP image processing
- Onboard microphone and speaker for two-way audio and intercom

**Active deterrents**

- Red & blue flashing light deterrents plus audible alerts
- 120dB Wide Dynamic Range for balanced exposure
- Includes mount

The EL-IP-4PT-WH can act as an intercom target for two-way talk from any Nice interface, local or remote, with the power of ELAN.
