## Description
### A 4-megapixel dome camera delivering best-in-class surveillance at its price point.

The Nice EL-IP-4FD is the dome form factor of the updated Nice surveillance camera line, bringing the latest advancements in camera technology including superior color night vision, deterrent technology, and an onboard mic and speaker. Paired with the Nice software NVR, it delivers a winning surveillance experience.

- 4MP resolution with fixed 2.8-4.0mm lens
- Color night vision powered by advanced AI-ISP image processing
- Onboard microphone and speaker for two-way audio and intercom
- Smart white light deterrent for full-color night capture

**Intelligent analytics**

- Onboard motion, tripwire, audio, and human & vehicle detection
- Precise target identification reduces false alarms
- 120dB Wide Dynamic Range for balanced exposure

The discreet dome housing suits ceiling installations while the EL-IP-4FD acts as an intercom target for two-way talk from any Nice interface, local or remote.
