## Description
### An 8-megapixel turret camera adding siren and strobe deterrents, improved night vision, and richer analytics.

The Nice EL-IP-8FT builds on the updated Nice surveillance camera line with higher 8MP resolution, improved low-light performance, and the added security of active deterrents. Combined with onboard mic and speaker and the Nice software NVR, it delivers a powerful and intelligent surveillance experience.

- 8MP resolution with fixed 2.8-4.0mm F1.0 lens for superior low-light capture
- Color night vision powered by advanced AI-ISP image processing
- Red & blue flashing light deterrents plus audible alerts
- Onboard microphone and speaker for two-way audio and intercom

**Intelligent analytics**

- Motion, tripwire, audio, human, vehicle, cycle detection and people counting
- 130dB Wide Dynamic Range for balanced exposure in challenging light
- Precise target identification to reduce false alarms

When an event is triggered, the camera can flash its onboard red and blue lights and broadcast an audible alert, warning intruders they are being watched.
