## Description
### High-resolution 8MP bullet surveillance with advanced deterrence and analytics.

The Nice EL-IP-8FB is the 8-megapixel bullet camera in the updated Nice surveillance line, adding siren and strobe deterrents, improved night vision, advanced analytics, and superior image quality over the 4MP models. Combined with the Nice software NVR, it delivers an intelligent, high-performance surveillance experience.

- 8MP resolution with fixed 2.8-4.0mm lens and bright F1.0 aperture
- Red & Blue flashing light deterrents with audible alarm broadcast
- Onboard microphone and speaker for two-way audio and intercom

**Advanced analytics**

- Motion, tripwire, audio, human, vehicle, cycle and people counting
- 130dB Wide Dynamic Range for superior exposure balance
- Color night vision with improved low-light performance

Available in black and white finishes with junction-box style mounts, the EL-IP-8FB acts as an intercom target for two-way talk from any Nice interface, local or remote.
