## Description
### The premium control center for the connected home.

The Nice EL-SC-350 is the premium control center for the connected home, bringing together all your intelligent devices to work as one seamless system. Control media, lighting, climate, pool/spa, irrigation, and more through a single app interface. As the flagship Nice controller, it gives you the ultimate flexibility for a system of virtually any size.

- Extensive connectivity: RS-232, RS-485, Relays, IR, IP, input sensors and Z-Wave control
- ARM big.LITTLE architecture integrating dual-core and quad-core processors
- HDMI 2.0 for 4K 60Hz output with HDCP 1.4/2.2 support and H.265 decoding up to 4K

**Built for any installation**

- Z-Wave control and automation of compatible devices built in
- Single rack space form factor with rack mount hardware included
- PoE ready and expandable with the EL-IO-200 Control Extender for large or decentralized installations

Consistent themes and layouts across Nice touch panels, remotes, and mobile apps deliver a multi-platform experience.
