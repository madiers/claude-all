## Description
### The new pinnacle of sound technology, pushing the envelope for ultra-high-performance subwoofers.

The Nice XTEQi-12 features dual 12" force-canceling active Kevlar drivers, each powered by a dedicated 1000W amplifier, with onboard DSP offering wireless setup and control via a mobile app. It accurately reproduces low frequencies down to 17Hz, delivering an experience that can be both felt and heard, ideal for music in listening rooms and motion picture content in home cinemas.

- Force-canceling dual active Kevlar High Back-EMF drivers in a perfectly opposed configuration
- High-gloss piano black lacquer finish over a 1" MDF cabinet
- Bi-amplified design with a dedicated 1000W amplifier per driver
- Optional wireless audio kit for flexible placement anywhere in a room

**Sub Station app and auto setup**

- Intuitive Sub Station app manages tuning and eliminates setup complexity
- Auto-EQ function with DSP room correction configures EQ at the push of a button
- Bluetooth Low Energy communication

Crafted to complement other high-end A/V components, the XTEQi-12 brings unprecedented sound performance and beauty to every environment.
