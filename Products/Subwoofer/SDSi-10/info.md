## Description
### Premium performance and unbeatable value in a compact 10-inch subwoofer.

The Nice SDSi-10 carries on the Sunfire subwoofer legacy, built on decades of bass innovation and patents. A triple-driver configuration pairs one 10" active polypropylene cone with dual 10" passive polypropylene radiators, driven by an exclusive Class-D amplifier for deep, immersive bass in a compact, hideaway cabinet wrapped in a beautiful matte black finish.

- Triple-driver configuration: single active driver with dual passive radiators
- Exclusive Class-D amplifier design
- Beautiful matte black finish cabinet
- Built-in 5V USB with integrated mount to support wireless audio
- Compact passive-radiator design keeps a smaller, hideaway profile

**Flexible placement**

- Optional wireless audio kit allows placement anywhere in a room
- Part of a multi-size SDSi series sized to complete any speaker system

From the moment it is installed, you will understand exactly why not all subwoofers are created equal.
