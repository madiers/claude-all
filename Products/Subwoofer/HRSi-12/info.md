## Description
### Design technology meets premium performance in a 12-inch subwoofer.

The Nice HRSi-12 takes the innovation of Sunfire subwoofers and builds on that legacy, crafting elegance and performance into a compact, space-efficient cabinet finished in distinguished 5-layer piano black lacquer. A 12" high-excursion, fiberglass-reinforced woofer using high motor-force EMF design pairs with a 1000W Class D amplifier for increased linear output with control.

- Ultra-compact power-to-size ratio
- 1000W high-output Class D amplifier
- Piano black lacquered 1" MDF cabinet
- DSP with digital room correction
- Built-in 5V USB for wireless audio adapter

**Sub Station app and auto setup**

- Streamlines subwoofer setup into a quick and easy process
- Auto-EQ function with digital room correction for flexible placement
- Bluetooth Low Energy communication

The result is best-in-class fidelity for any source of audio.
