## Description
### A legacy of audio science and technology, re-crafted for in-wall installations.

The Nice HRSiW8 (SC-HRSiW8-CAB) is a 520-watt, vibration-free in-wall subwoofer designed to operate as a system with its dedicated rack-mounted amplifier (HRSIW8AMP, sold separately). Wrapped in a cabinet so thin and sophisticated you can tap into all that power with virtually no mechanical vibration, it reproduces movies, music, and games with accurate, even-handed bass.

- Two active 8" High Back EMF drivers with fiberglass cones for the most accurate bass
- Sealed, high-pressure cabinet design built exclusively for in-wall applications
- Custom Digital Audio Adjustment for professional-grade, custom sound profiles
- Pre-programmed remote control to switch sound modes at the push of a button

**Proprietary technology**

- StillBass anti-shake technology eliminates mechanical noises for clean, crisp bass
- Frequency Filtration Design (FFD) in the matching amplifier perfectly pairs to the subwoofer
- DSP high-cut filter adjustable 40-160 Hz with LFE bypass and 12-36 dB/octave slope

The subwoofer, amplifier, and remote are purpose-built to work together harmoniously, each component complementing the other for the best possible performance.
