## Description
### Contractor ten-pack of single Blink-IR emitters with visible command confirmation.

The 283D10P is a ten-pack of the 283D Blink-IR Designer Emitter. Each emitter houses a small infrared LED in a miniature, designer-styled, black injection-molded plastic shell and emits visible red light in addition to its infrared control signals when activated by IR commands from IR receivers or other controllers. Each is designed to mount directly on the IR sensor window of the controlled device.

- Bulk pack of ten emitters for multi-component and multi-room installs
- Emits visible red light alongside IR signals for at-a-glance command confirmation
- IR-transparent shell allows handheld remote commands to pass straight through it

**Key details**

- 3.5mm mono mini plug per emitter, approximately 10 ft cable length
- White-striped side of the plug is positive (+)
- Connects to the EMITTERS or OUT jacks on connecting blocks and controllers

Each emitter also flags stray IR or RF interference by randomly blinking when no control signal is being sent. The optional 28DES shield is available for zone-controlled applications.
