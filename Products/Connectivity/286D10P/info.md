## Description
### Two emitters on one plug, each blinking red to confirm IR activity.

The 286D10P is a ten-pack of the 286D Dual Blink-IR Designer Emitter. Each cable carries two emitter heads housing small infrared LEDs in miniature, designer-styled, black injection-molded plastic shells. Unlike standard emitters, the 286D emits visible red light in addition to its infrared control signals when activated by commands from IR receivers or other controllers. The two emitters are wired in series and mount directly on the IR sensor windows of the controlled components.

- Dual emitter heads on one plug control two components at once
- Emits visible red light alongside IR signals for at-a-glance command confirmation
- Emitters wired in series for installation on two controlled devices

**Key details**

- Single 3.5mm mono mini plug feeding two emitter heads
- White-striped side of the plug is positive (+)
- Connects to the EMITTERS or OUT jacks on connecting blocks and controllers

Each emitter also flags stray IR or RF interference by randomly blinking when no control signal is being sent, helping installers reposition interfering sources. The optional 28DES shield is available for zone-controlled applications.
