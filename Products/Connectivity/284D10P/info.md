## Description
### One emitter cable that controls two components from a single port.

The 284D10P is a ten-pack of the 284D Dual Designer Emitter. Each cable carries two emitter heads, each containing a tiny infrared LED in a miniature, designer-styled, black injection-molded plastic shell. They emit infrared control signals sent to them by IR receivers or other controllers, and can be installed directly on the IR sensor windows of two controlled devices or other suitable nearby locations.

- Dual emitter heads on one plug control two components at once
- High output from the curved top side permits control from up to 3 feet away on-axis
- Flexible placement above, below, or in front of components for cleaner aesthetics

**Key details**

- Single 3.5mm mono mini plug feeding two emitter heads
- White-striped side of the plug is positive (+)
- Connects to the EMITTERS or OUT jacks on connecting blocks and controllers

Unlike the Blink-IR models, the 284D is a non-blinking emitter (no visible red output). The optional 28DES shield is available for zone-controlled applications.
