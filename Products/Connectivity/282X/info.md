## Description
### A compact micro IR emitter for discreet, single-component control.

The 282X is a single micro IR emitter from the Xantech designer emitter family. It houses an infrared LED in a small, low-profile shell that mounts directly over the IR sensor window of a controlled component, relaying control signals from connected IR receivers, connecting blocks, or controllers.

- Compact, low-profile emitter head for discreet placement
- Mounts directly over a component's IR sensor window with its adhesive base
- Relays IR commands from connecting blocks and controllers via a mono mini plug

**Key details**

- 3.5mm mono mini plug connects to the EMITTERS / OUT jacks of compatible connecting blocks and controllers
- Single-emitter design for controlling one component per port

The 282X is part of the same designer emitter system as the 283D, 284D and 286D and is compatible with the optional 28DES IR-opaque shield for zone-controlled applications.
