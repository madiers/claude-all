## Description
### Block stray IR so equipment responds only to its designer emitter.

The 28DES is a black, IR-opaque 1" x 2" shield that fits over both the Designer Emitter body and the IR sensor window of a controlled component. An optional accessory, it prevents unwanted external IR signals from passing through or leaking past it, isolating the controlled device from interference.

- Blocks external IR from reaching the controlled component's sensor
- Prevents IR from the designer emitter radiating backward into other components
- Ideal for zone-controlled installations where each device is driven only by its emitter

**Installation**

- Fit the shield and emitter together and position over the IR sensor window before removing adhesive covers
- Trim the shield as needed to overlap the edges of the sensor window
- Remove the adhesive covers and stick the shield and emitter onto the window

The 28DES pairs with the 283D, 284D, 286D and other designer emitters to deliver reliable, interference-free IR control in multi-component racks and multi-zone systems.
