## Description
### Answer the door from anywhere with the Nice Video Doorbell.

Pair the Nice Video Doorbell with the award-winning Nice Home Management system to answer the door from anywhere in the home with ITP panels and HR40 remotes, or anywhere in the world on mobile devices. With a low profile, sleek understated design and black anodized aluminum finish, it fits elegantly with any front door decor.

- Wide angle 1080P HD camera with automatic lighting
- Automatic motion notifications whenever movement is detected
- Seamlessly integrates with surveillance NVRs for recording and full playback through Nice interfaces

**Easy to install and connect**

- Auto Discover / Auto Configure with Nice Home Management
- EL-DB-WP connects via PoE or Wi-Fi with 12VDC power; EL-DB-2W connects with 2 wires for power and Ethernet
- IP65 rated weather protection with included angle mounting brackets

A built-in relay output and two configurable inputs, plus compatibility with 3rd party NVR and control systems, make it a versatile entry solution.
