## Description
### Best-in-class deep bass through a small, finished ceiling opening.

The SC-IC-SUB8 high-performance small-aperture in-ceiling subwoofer delivers best-in-class deep bass through a discreet 4.75" finished opening to meet the aesthetic needs of the modern home. The woofer cabinet hides between ceiling joists, porting low-frequency sound through the small opening. It features a custom 8.27" polypropylene woofer, dual 1.5" voice coils, a precision-tuned port tube, and a vinyl-wrapped MDF cabinet mounted to a single joist.

- Custom 8.27" polypropylene cone maximized to the cabinet proportions
- Dual voice coil for easy stereo wiring or single-channel use with included shorting bars
- Analog filtering circuits with a massive 10 mH inductor per voice coil for deep, clear bass
- 4.75" cut-out size installs with a standard hole saw
- Simple single-joist mounting with integrated absorption pads to reduce kinetic transmission

**Designed as a system**

- Engineered in the USA in tandem with the SC-IC3 speaker and SC4-100 amplifier
- Delivers three-way, full-range sound optimized for aesthetics, performance, and flexibility
