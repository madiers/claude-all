## Description
### Add Dante network audio to Nice IP amplifiers and pre-amps.

The Nice EL-IPD-NAC-EXT Network Audio Card enables the Nice EL-IPD-MTX-8CH Multi Zone Audio Matrix and the Nice EL-IPD-PRE-MSI Multi Zone Preamp Audio Matrix to connect to other Nice Dante-enabled devices. Nice IP-enabled audio distribution simplifies whole-house audio and enhances the intelligent home by delivering high-quality audio over the existing network.

- Adds Dante audio networking to compatible Nice IP chassis
- Compatible with the EL-IPD-MTX-8CH matrix amplifier and EL-IPD-PRE-MSI pre-amp matrix
- Single RJ45 jack with 100/1000 Ethernet, DHCP enabled by default

**Scalability**

- Connects sources to and from other Dante-enabled Nice IP devices
- Enables scaling from a single chassis to decentralized multi-chassis, multi-source systems
- Supports projects of up to 64 individual zones

The Network Audio Card is the bridge that turns a standalone Nice matrix or pre-amp into a node on a networked, decentralized audio distribution system.
