## Description
### Add up to eight zones of amplified and pre-amp audio over the network.

The Nice EL-IPD-AMP-EXT is used in conjunction with an amplifier or pre-amp in the Nice family of IP-enabled amps to add up to 8 zones of audio. Speaker outputs configure as left, right, or mono with single or multiple speakers per output, while four line-level outputs configure as left, right, mono, or subwoofer out. Each output or combination of outputs can be set up as a separate zone with EQ and lip-sync delay applied individually.

- 4 stereo / 8 mono amplified zones plus 2 stereo / 4 mono pre-amp zones
- 80W per channel at 8 ohm, 100W per channel at 4 ohm
- Dante audio networking with DSP and 5-band EQ for each zone

**Configuration and integration**

- Three 3.5mm mono zone/system trigger outputs (+12Vdc, 100mA)
- Individual lip-sync delay per speaker and pre-amp zone, 0-400ms
- Tone control per zone: five-band EQ, loudness, high/low pass, adjustable crossover

Nice IP-enabled audio distribution simplifies whole-house audio and scales to projects of up to 64 individual zones. Note: the EL-IPD-AMP-EXT cannot operate standalone; it requires another Dante-enabled Nice amplifier or pre-amp.
