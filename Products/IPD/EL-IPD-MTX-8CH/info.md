## Description
### A standalone matrix amplifier that scales to a networked multi-zone system.

The Nice EL-IPD-MTX-8CH amplifier can be used in standalone mode to support up to 13 input sources (4 digital, 8 mono / 4 stereo analog RCA, and 1 stereo 3.5mm doorbell input from a Nice System Controller). Each RCA analog input can be paired as L/R or used individually as mono. Eight speaker channels provide up to 4 stereo or 8 mono zones, and four line-level outputs configure as up to 4 mono or 2 stereo zones or subwoofer out. The MTX-8CH replaces the S86A.

- 4 stereo / 8 mono amplified zones plus 2 stereo / 4 mono pre-amp zones
- 2 optical, 2 coax, and 4 stereo / 8 mono analog inputs
- 80W per channel at 8 ohm, 100W per channel at 4 ohm

**Expansion and DSP**

- Expandable with additional chassis and the optional EL-IPD-NAC-EXT Dante network audio card
- DSP with 5-band EQ, lip-sync correction, and adjustable crossover per zone
- Dedicated 3.5mm stereo doorbell input

Adding the optional Dante audio networking card connects sources to and from other Dante-enabled Nice IP amplifiers, scaling to projects of up to 64 individual zones.
