## Description
### High-quality audio to a single zone or as part of a multi-zone system.

The Nice EL-IPD-AMP-2CH 2 Channel Audio Amplifier can be used in a single-chassis configuration or alongside other amplifiers and pre-amps within the Nice IP audio distribution family using Dante audio networking. Nice IP-enabled audio distribution simplifies whole-house audio and enhances the intelligent home by delivering high-quality audio over an existing network, scaling from a single chassis to a decentralized multi-chassis, multi-source implementation.

- Two stereo amplifier outputs, bridgeable to 200W in bridge mode
- 80W per channel at 8 ohm, 100W per channel at 4 ohm
- Optical TOSLINK, coaxial RCA, and stereo analog RCA inputs

**DSP and zone control**

- Individual lip-sync delay per speaker zone, 0-400ms in 10ms increments
- Individual tone control per zone with five-band equalizer and loudness on/off
- Subwoofer output that follows the amplifier's main volume

Connected over 100/1000 Ethernet with DHCP enabled by default, the EL-IPD-AMP-2CH integrates cleanly into ELAN control systems and Dante networks for flexible whole-home audio.
