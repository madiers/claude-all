## Description
### Effortless, high-quality sound for every room.

The Nice SC1-IC6 is a Series 1 premium toolless in-ceiling speaker optimized for high-quality ambient music and everyday media applications. It pairs a 6.5 inch injected polypropylene woofer with a 1 inch (25mm) silk dome tweeter to deliver clean, balanced sound throughout the home.

- 6.5 inch injected polypropylene woofer for warm, reliable output
- 1 inch (25mm) silk dome tweeter for smooth high-frequency detail
- XMag longer pole piece maintains a stable magnetic field for precise linearity

**ViseLock toolless installation**

- ViseLock mounting system designed by Swarm enables secure installs up to 75% faster than conventional dog-leg systems
- Electroplated painted steel grill resists rust and is paintable to match any ceiling

The SC1-IC6 is available in TILT, dual-tweeter (DT), and all-weather (AW) variants, and accepts the optional FRBC-UNI 1-hour fire-rated speaker cover for fire-rated ceiling assemblies. Backed by a 5 year warranty.
