## Description
### Reference-grade in-wall LCR performance for home theater.

The Nice SC3-IW5-LCR is a flagship Series 3 premium toolless in-wall speaker configured for left, center, and right (LCR) channel use in high-end home theaters. Dual 5.25 inch black Kevlar woofers flank an oversized aluminum/magnesium dome tweeter for explosive dynamics and a precise, detailed soundstage.

- Dual 5.25 inch black Kevlar woofers in an MTM-style layout for explosive dynamics
- Proportionally massive 1 1/4 inch (30mm) aluminum/magnesium dome tweeter lowers the crossover point for exceptional imagery
- XMag longer pole piece ensures precise linearity and accurate reproduction

**AccuAim and ViseLock technologies**

- Proprietary AccuAim aiming enables precise, lockable tweeter positioning for enhanced phase accuracy
- ViseLock toolless mounting system designed by Swarm enables installs up to 75% faster than conventional systems
- Electroplated painted steel grill resists rust and is paintable to match any wall

Backed by a 5 year warranty.
