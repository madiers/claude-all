## Description
### Cinematic in-wall performance for left, center, and right channels.

The Nice SC2-IW5-LCR is a Series 2 premium toolless in-wall speaker configured for left, center, and right (LCR) channel use in home theater and immersive media systems. Dual 5.25 inch anodized ceramic/aluminum woofers flank a central tweeter for wide dispersion and dynamic, detailed sound.

- Dual 5.25 inch anodized ceramic/aluminum woofers in an MTM-style layout
- 1 inch (25mm) anodized ceramic/aluminum dome tweeter for accurate high-frequency detail
- XMag longer pole piece ensures precise linearity and accurate reproduction

**ViseLock toolless installation**

- ViseLock mounting system designed by Swarm enables installs up to 75% faster than conventional systems
- Electroplated painted steel grill resists rust and is paintable to match any wall

Backed by a 5 year warranty.
