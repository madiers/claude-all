## Description
### Premium performance for elevated audio experiences.

The Nice SC3-IC6 is a flagship Series 3 premium toolless in-ceiling speaker delivering reference-grade audio for home theaters, dedicated listening rooms, and high-end whole-home audio. A black Kevlar 6.5 inch woofer and an oversized aluminum/magnesium dome tweeter combine for explosive dynamics and a rich, detailed soundstage.

- 6.5 inch black Kevlar woofer for explosive dynamics and a rich soundstage
- Proportionally massive 1 1/4 inch (30mm) aluminum/magnesium dome tweeter lowers the crossover point for exceptional imagery
- XMag longer pole piece ensures precise linearity and accurate reproduction

**AccuAim and ViseLock technologies**

- Proprietary AccuAim aiming enables precise, lockable tweeter positioning for enhanced phase accuracy
- ViseLock toolless mounting system designed by Swarm enables installs up to 75% faster than conventional systems
- Electroplated painted steel grill resists rust and is paintable to match any ceiling

Available in a TILT variant and compatible with the optional FRBC-UNI 1-hour fire-rated cover. Backed by a 5 year warranty.
