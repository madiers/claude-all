## Description
### Reference-grade in-ceiling sound with deep, powerful bass.

The Nice SC3-IC8 is the largest single-woofer model in the flagship Series 3 line, pairing an 8 inch black Kevlar woofer with an oversized aluminum/magnesium dome tweeter for the deepest low-end and highest output in the family. It is engineered for premium home theaters and elevated listening spaces.

- 8 inch black Kevlar woofer reaching down to 36 Hz for powerful, articulate bass
- Proportionally massive 1 1/4 inch (30mm) aluminum/magnesium dome tweeter lowers the crossover point for exceptional imagery
- XMag longer pole piece ensures precise linearity and accurate reproduction

**AccuAim and ViseLock technologies**

- Proprietary AccuAim aiming enables precise, lockable tweeter positioning for enhanced phase accuracy
- ViseLock toolless mounting system designed by Swarm enables installs up to 75% faster than conventional systems
- Electroplated painted steel grill resists rust and is paintable to match any ceiling

Available in dual-tweeter (DT) and TILT variants and compatible with the optional FRBC-UNI 1-hour fire-rated cover. Backed by a 5 year warranty.
