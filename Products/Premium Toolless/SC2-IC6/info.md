## Description
### Immersive sound for gaming, streaming, and distributed audio.

The Nice SC2-IC6 is a Series 2 premium toolless in-ceiling speaker built for immersive gaming, music streaming, and whole-home distributed audio. Its anodized ceramic/aluminum 6.5 inch woofer and matching anodized ceramic/aluminum dome tweeter deliver crisp, dynamic, detailed sound.

- 6.5 inch anodized ceramic/aluminum woofer for explosive dynamics
- 1 inch (25mm) deep anodized ceramic/aluminum dome tweeter for accurate detail
- XMag longer pole piece ensures precise linearity and accurate reproduction

**ViseLock toolless installation**

- ViseLock mounting system designed by Swarm enables installs up to 75% faster than conventional systems
- Electroplated painted steel grill resists rust and is paintable to match any ceiling

Available in a TILT variant and compatible with the optional FRBC-UNI 1-hour fire-rated cover. Backed by a 5 year warranty.
