## Description

### A 4x4 HDBaseT matrix offering unprecedented performance and value.

The Nice EL-4KM-V44-18G is an HDMI 2.0 4K 60Hz 4:4:4, HDCP 2.2 matrix that uses CSC technology to deliver HDMI, bi-directional IR and PoC up to 70m over a single CAT cable. It is built for the custom installation market, with advanced features including simultaneous HDBaseT/HDMI on output 1 and video down-conversion on the HDBaseT outputs.

- Four HDMI inputs independently routable to four HDBaseT outputs
- Output 1 features simultaneous HDMI and HDBaseT output
- Advanced Color Space Conversion (CSC) supports the HDMI 2.0 18Gbps specification including HDR

**Distribution and control**

- Supports 4K 60Hz 4:4:4 UHD video up to 40m, and 1080p video up to 70m
- Video down-conversion on HDBaseT outputs so lower-resolution displays still receive content while other outputs keep full 4K UHD
- Supports all industry-standard resolutions (VGA-WUXGA, 480i-4K) and all known HDMI audio formats including Dolby TrueHD, Dolby Atmos, Dolby Digital Plus and DTS-HD Master Audio
- Control via front panel, IR, RS-232 and TCP/IP, with a built-in web browser interface
- Supports PoC to power compatible HDBaseT receivers; advanced EDID management and HDCP 2.2 compliant

A 1U rack-mountable chassis with included rack ears, supplied with a remote control, IR emitters/receivers, control cables and four regional IEC power cables.
