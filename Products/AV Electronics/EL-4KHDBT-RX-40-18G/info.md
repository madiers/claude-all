## Description

### Extend HDMI, powered by the matrix, up to 70m (4K to 40m).

The Nice EL-4KHDBT-RX-40-18G is an HDMI 2.0 4K 60Hz 4:4:4 (18Gbps), HDCP 2.2 HDBaseT ARC receiver that delivers market-leading features and outstanding value using CSC technology. It extends HDMI, bi-directional IR and RS-232 over a single CAT cable and is powered by the matrix.

- Extends 4K UHD video up to 40m (3840 x 2160 @30Hz 4:4:4, 4096 x 2160 @24Hz 4:4:4, and 4K @60Hz)
- Extends 1080p video up to 70m
- HDMI loop output for integrating a local display or cascading to multiple devices

**Audio and control**

- ARC support when used with compatible Nice control system hardware
- Simultaneous analog stereo and optical Toslink audio breakout
- Bi-directional RS-232 and IR pass-through
- Supports PoC to receive power from HDBaseT transmitters

Supplied with a mounting bracket set and quick reference guide for fast custom installation.
