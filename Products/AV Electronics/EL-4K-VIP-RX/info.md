## Description
### Premium 4K video distribution over standard 1Gb network architecture.

The Nice EL-4K-VIP-RX is an HDCP 2.2 compliant Video over IP (VIP) receiver that uses visually lossless compression technology to deliver HDMI, bi-directional IR, RS-232 and USB/KVM up to 100m over a standard network. It pairs with the EL-4K-VIP-TX transmitter and can be powered via PoE or locally where a switch does not support PoE, making it ideal for both residential and commercial installations.

- Advanced UHD video over a 1Gb network with virtually unlimited system size
- Supports up to 4K HDR UHD video (3840 x 2160 @30Hz 4:4:4, 4096 x 2160 @24Hz 4:4:4, and 4K @60Hz 4:2:0)
- Supports all known HDMI audio formats including Dolby TrueHD, Dolby Digital Plus and DTS-HD Master Audio

**Flexible operation**

- Four operational modes: matrix distribution, video wall, one-to-one HDMI extender, and one-to-many HDMI splitter
- Analog L/R audio breakout, video up- and down-scaling, and advanced EDID management
- Bi-directional RS-232 and IR pass-through plus USB 2.0 connectivity for KVM and other devices
- HDMI audio embedding up to 192 kHz; analog audio output up to 96 kHz

Simple configuration via the web GUI through the EL-4K-VIP-ACM, with RS-232, IR and Telnet/IP integration for full system control.
