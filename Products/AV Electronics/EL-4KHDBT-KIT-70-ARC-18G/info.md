## Description

### Outstanding value HDBaseT extension powered up to 100m (4K to 70m).

The Nice EL-4KHDBT-KIT-70-ARC-18G is an HDMI 2.0 4K 60Hz 4:4:4 (18Gbps), HDCP 2.2 HDBaseT ARC extender kit that uses CSC technology to deliver uncompressed video and audio with zero latency. The matched transmitter (EL-4KHDBT-TX-70-ARC-18G) and receiver (EL-4KHDBT-RX-70-ARC-18G) extend HDMI, bi-directional IR and RS-232 over a single CAT cable.

- Advanced HDBaseT technology delivering uncompressed video and audio with zero latency
- Extends HDMI 4K60 4:4:4 up to 70m, and 1080p up to 100m, over a single CAT cable
- Supports 4K UHD video (3840 x 2160 @30Hz 4:4:4, 4096 x 2160 @24Hz 4:4:4, and 4K @60Hz 4:2:0)

**Audio, control and power**

- Supports ARC (Audio Return Channel) from the connected display via HDMI ARC or optical digital input
- Supports all known HDMI audio formats including Dolby TrueHD, Dolby Atmos, Dolby Digital Plus and DTS-HD Master Audio
- Bi-directional IR and RS-232 pass-through, supplied with a Nice IR receiver and emitter
- Bi-directional PoC (Power over Cable) from either the transmitter or receiver end; HDCP 2.2 compliant with smart EDID management

When using an eARC display, only 2-channel PCM audio is supported. Supplied with a 24V/1.25A power supply, IR emitter, IR receiver, mounting bracket set and quick reference guide.
