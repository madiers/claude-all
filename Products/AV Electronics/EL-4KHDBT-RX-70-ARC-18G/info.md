## Description

### Uncompressed 4K HDBaseT reception with ARC and audio breakout.

The Nice EL-4KHDBT-RX-70-ARC-18G is the HDBaseT ARC receiver from the Nice 4K HDBaseT extender platform, delivering HDMI 2.0 4K 60Hz 4:4:4 (18Gbps), HDCP 2.2 video with zero latency over a single CAT cable using CSC technology. It receives uncompressed video and audio extended up to 70m at 4K.

- Receives 4K UHD video up to 70m (3840 x 2160 @30Hz 4:4:4, 4096 x 2160 @24Hz 4:4:4, and 4K @60Hz 4:2:0)
- Supports ARC (Audio Return Channel) from a connected display via HDMI ARC or optical digital input
- Optical (S/PDIF) and analog L/R audio breakout

**Control and power**

- Bi-directional IR and RS-232 pass-through
- Supports bi-directional PoC (Power over Cable) from either the transmitter or receiver end
- HDCP 2.2 compliant with smart EDID management

Supports all known HDMI audio formats including Dolby TrueHD, Dolby Atmos, Dolby Digital Plus and DTS-HD Master Audio. When used with an eARC display, only 2-channel PCM audio is supported.
