## Description
### Impressive audio quality from patio to poolside.

Nice Terrazza is a value-priced outdoor speaker system that over-delivers on performance. The SC-TERR-4-1 kit pairs four weatherproof 4" Terrazza satellite speakers with a single 8" dual voice coil subwoofer — available as a buried subwoofer (SC-TERR-4.1-BU) for the flowerbed or an above-ground hardscape subwoofer (SC-TERR-4.1-HS) for the deck. Unlike high-performance landscape systems that require expensive high-power amplifiers, Terrazza is designed for lower-powered distributed audio amplifiers or extended AVR zones, and expands up to an 8.2 system.

- Four 4" weatherproof satellites with polypropylene cone woofer and 3/4" titanium dome tweeter
- One 8" DVC subwoofer in a low-noise rotational-molded polyethylene enclosure with 2nd-order crossover
- Up to 150W (max) power handling; designed for distributed audio amplifiers or AVRs

**Easy, toolless installation**

- Quick-connect satellite design for thread-free attachment, plus post-install rotation and tilt via thumbscrew
- Handy hammer cap protects the stake while driving it into the ground
- Silicone-filled wire connectors included for all connections

Frequency response 34Hz-20kHz (BU subwoofer) or 38Hz-20kHz (HS subwoofer). Expand to an 8.2 configuration by combining kits or adding SC-TERR-2.0 satellite packs.
