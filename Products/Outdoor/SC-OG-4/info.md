## Description
### Bold outdoor performance with long-lasting durability.

The Nice SC-OG-4 brings the bold audio performance of Nice architectural speakers to outdoor spaces of all sizes. Its compact 4" satellite form factor blends discreetly into any landscape while delivering the Nice signature sound throughout the yard.

- Weather-resistant fiberglass-reinforced ABS enclosure built to withstand harsh climates
- 110-degree wide sound dispersion from a 2-way design for clear, even coverage
- 8 Ohm or 70V/100V operation for flexible system scaling

**Versatile installation**

- Selectable 70V/100V tap settings allow individual volume control of every speaker
- Quick-connect stake, surface and conduit mounting accessories available (sold separately)
- Rugged enough to stand up to outdoor garden equipment, including weed whackers

A space-saving landscape satellite that pairs with Nice in-ground subwoofers for full-range outdoor sound.
