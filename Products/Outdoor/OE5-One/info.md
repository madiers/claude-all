## Description
### Modest sound requirements with wonderful performance.

The Nice OE5 One Outdoor Elements speaker addresses a multitude of exterior applications and is available in black or white. A single 5.25" IMG cone woofer loaded into a ported enclosure dramatically enhances low-frequency response for deep, full bass that complements any type of music.

- One 5.25" IMG cone woofer with rubber surround for full, deep bass
- One pivoting 1" liquid-cooled treated silk dome tweeter
- Ported enclosure that enhances low-frequency response

**Built for the outdoors**

- Drivers made from space-age polymers, injection-molded graphite or aluminum for sonic purity
- Timbre-matched across all three Outdoor Elements series speakers
- 4 Ohm impedance with 100W power handling

A compact, high-fidelity indoor/outdoor speaker ideal for patios, decks and covered exterior spaces.
