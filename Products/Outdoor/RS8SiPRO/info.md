## Description
### Designed to be heard and not seen.

The Nice RS8SiPRO is the flagship of the RS PRO rock loudspeaker series, blending into virtually any outdoor environment with a GeoRealistic finish and NoFade color. Its dual voice coil (DVC) woofer can play full stereo from a single cabinet when wired in stereo-input mode, covering large areas with high-quality stereo imaging. The proprietary ConeForward design positions the drivers close to the grille to markedly improve midrange intelligibility.

- 8" dual voice coil injection-molded TCC polypropylene woofer for superior, room-filling bass
- Dual 1" fluid-cooled Teteron tweeters angled twenty degrees from each other for wide dispersion
- Dual precision crossovers keep left and right channels fully isolated for excellent stereo imaging

**Weather tested, Mother Nature approved**

- Custom multi-layer fiberglass cabinet with double seals on tweeters, woofer and enclosure cover
- Low-diffraction MicroPerf aluminum grilles resist oxidation in the harshest conditions
- Withstands extreme temperatures from -50 to 185°F (-45 to 85°C); includes security bracket and cable

Available in Granite, Sandstone, Speckled Granite and Shale Brown. Presents a 4 Ohm load in single-speaker stereo mode and 8 Ohm in two-speaker stereo mode.
