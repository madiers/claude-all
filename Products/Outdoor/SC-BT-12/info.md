## Description
### Bold outdoor performance with long-lasting durability.

Complete your outdoor listening experience and hear the rocking bass you've been missing with the Nice SC-BT-12 Landscape Subwoofer. Built from the same material used to distribute municipal water underground, its waterproof enclosure buries discreetly, leaving only a low-profile canopy that blends into the surroundings. The cabinet and port design are computer-optimized to deliver deep, distortion-free bass that fills your outdoor space.

- 12" mica-injected polypropylene woofer for deep, powerful low end
- Waterproof buried HDPE enclosure dunk-tested before it ships
- Up to 500W power handling with a low-impedance 8 Ohm design

**Built for the elements**

- High-flow port and canopy assembly spreads air in a 360-degree pattern to eliminate port noise
- Critter-proof aluminum mesh grille keeps water, dirt, bugs and animals out
- Discreet canopy blends naturally into landscaping

The largest of the Nice landscape subwoofers, ideal for filling expansive outdoor areas with full-range sound.
