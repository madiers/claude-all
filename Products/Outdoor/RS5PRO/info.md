## Description
### Designed to be heard and not seen.

The Nice RS5PRO is the most compact model in the RS PRO rock loudspeaker series, blending into virtually any outdoor environment with a GeoRealistic finish and NoFade color. Like the rest of the RS PRO line, it uses an injection-molded TCC polypropylene woofer and a fluid-cooled Teteron tweeter paired with shaped low-diffraction MicroPerf aluminum grilles. The proprietary ConeForward design positions the drivers close to the grille to markedly improve midrange intelligibility.

- 5.25" injection-molded TCC (talc, carbon, ceramic-filled) polypropylene woofer
- 1" fluid-cooled Teteron tweeter angled twenty degrees up toward the listener
- ConeForward driver placement for improved midrange clarity and imaging

**Weather tested, Mother Nature approved**

- Custom multi-layer fiberglass cabinet with double seals on tweeter, woofer and enclosure cover
- Rust-resistant MicroPerf aluminum grilles painted on both sides
- Withstands extreme temperatures from -50 to 185°F (-45 to 85°C); includes security bracket and cable

A compact rock speaker for smaller landscapes, available in Granite, Sandstone, Speckled Granite and Shale Brown.
