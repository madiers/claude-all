## Description
### Unmatched high fidelity in a smaller package.

The Nice OE6 One Outdoor Elements speaker addresses a multitude of exterior applications and is available in black or white. A single 6.5" IMG cone woofer loaded into a ported enclosure dramatically enhances low-frequency response for deep, full bass that complements any type of music.

- One 6.5" IMG cone woofer with rubber surround for deep, full bass
- One pivoting 1" liquid-cooled treated silk dome tweeter
- Ported enclosure that enhances low-frequency response

**Built for the outdoors**

- Drivers made from space-age polymers, injection-molded graphite or aluminum for sonic purity
- Timbre-matched across all three Outdoor Elements series speakers
- 4 Ohm impedance with 125W power handling

A versatile, full-bodied indoor/outdoor speaker for patios, pergolas and covered exterior spaces.
