## Description
### Provide both the required sound pressure levels and musicality in all exterior situations.

The Nice OE8 Outdoor Elements speaker addresses a multitude of exterior applications and is available in black or white. A single 8" aluminum cone woofer loaded into a ported enclosure dramatically enhances low-frequency response, delivering deep, full bass that complements any type of music.

- One 8" aluminum cone woofer with rubber surround for high-output, deep bass
- One 2.5" aluminum dome midrange for clear, detailed vocals
- One pivoting 1" liquid-cooled aluminum dome tweeter

**Built for the outdoors**

- Three-way design delivers both high sound pressure levels and musicality
- Drivers made from space-age polymers, injection-molded graphite or aluminum for sonic purity
- Timbre-matched across all three Outdoor Elements series speakers; 4 Ohm, 150W power handling

The flagship Outdoor Elements speaker, ideal for larger patios and exterior spaces that demand serious output.
