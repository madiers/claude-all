## Description
### Designed to be heard and not seen.

The Nice RS6 rock loudspeaker blends seamlessly into virtually any outdoor environment while bringing quality sound to your landscape. Years of engineering went into a natural look with GeoRealistic finishes and NoFade color, paired with shaped low-diffraction MicroPerf aluminum grilles for an acoustically transparent, realistic appearance. The proprietary ConeForward design positions the drivers close to the grille to markedly improve midrange intelligibility.

- 6.5" injection-molded TCC (talc, carbon, ceramic-filled) polypropylene woofer for superior bass
- 1" fluid-cooled Teteron tweeter angled twenty degrees up toward the listener
- ConeForward driver placement for improved midrange clarity and imaging

**Weather tested, Mother Nature approved**

- Custom multi-layer fiberglass cabinet with double seals on tweeter, woofer and enclosure cover
- Rust-resistant MicroPerf aluminum grilles painted on both sides
- Withstands extreme temperatures from -50 to 185°F (-45 to 85°C)

Available in Granite, Sandstone, Speckled Granite and Shale Brown to disappear into any landscape.
