## Description
### Designed to be heard and not seen.

The Nice PS6Si planter loudspeaker brings quality sound to outdoor spaces while doubling as a working planter that can be used with live plants. Finished in weathered concrete with a GeoRealistic look, it disappears into any patio or garden. A dual voice coil woofer lets a single cabinet play full stereo, and an innovative movable tweeter assembly adjusts dispersion from 180° for open areas down to 90° for corners.

- 6.5" dual voice coil injection-molded TCC polypropylene woofer for superior bass
- Movable tweeter assembly adjusts dispersion from 180° (open areas) to 90° (corners)
- ConeForward driver placement for improved midrange intelligibility

**Weather tested, Mother Nature approved**

- Custom multi-layer fiberglass cabinet with double-sealed drivers and enclosure
- Low-diffraction MicroPerf aluminum grilles resist oxidation in harsh conditions
- Withstands extreme temperatures from -50 to 185°F (-45 to 85°C)

A weathered-concrete planter speaker that plays stereo from one cabinet and holds live plants on top. Presents a 4 Ohm load when L/R is summed in parallel.
