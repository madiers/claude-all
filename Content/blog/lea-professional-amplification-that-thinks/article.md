# LEA Professional — Amplification that thinks

_(hero: images/1-hero.png)_

LEA Professional set out to reinvent the most overlooked box in the rack: the amplifier. The result — the Connect Series — is a family of networked, self-monitoring smart amplifiers that install in minutes, manage from a browser, and quietly look after themselves. In 2026 the range took **Best of Show at InfoComm**.

## The smart amplifier

Every channel carries onboard DSP — EQ, limiting, routing and matrix mixing — so processing lives inside the amp rather than in another box. Setup happens in any web browser with no software to install, and each unit is IoT-connected for monitoring and management from anywhere in the world.

_(images/2-connect-series.png)_

## Dante, on the network

On the "D" models, audio travels over standard IP via Dante — with redundant Primary and Secondary streams for mission-critical installs. Fewer cables, cleaner racks, and a signal path that scales with the network instead of the copper.

## Built for the install

- 4- and 8-channel models, from 80 W to 3000 W per channel
- 70V / 100V / Lo-Z direct-drive, switchable per output
- 1U chassis, universal power supply, energy-efficient Class-D

One platform covers everything from a boardroom to a stadium.

## Recognised at InfoComm 2026

The Connect Series earned AV Technology's **Best of Show at InfoComm 2026** — recognition for pushing networked, self-aware amplification into the mainstream.
