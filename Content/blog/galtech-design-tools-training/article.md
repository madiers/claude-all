## Prove even sound, on screen

The headline demo is simple: draw the room, drop in speakers, and turn on the SPL heatmap. The client watches their venue fill with even, measured sound, and you have a conversation your competition cannot have.

![A live SPL heatmap across two rooms, with speaker coverage](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/madiers/claude-all/claude/blissful-cori-JY3Gi/Content/blog/galtech-design-tools-training/images/3-spl-coverage.png)

Behind the colour is real acoustics. The tool estimates reverberation from the room size and surface materials, honours the venue background noise, and grades your design against professional standards: coverage, uniformity, speech clarity and headroom. A failed clarity check even becomes a sellable line item, because the treatment advisor tells you how many square metres of absorption to add.

## Close the deal in 3D

The heatmap is the technical proof. The 3D sound test is the emotional close. Your client sees their actual room, the speakers where they will really go, and the coverage under their feet, and they walk it in first person with your logo on the welcome screen.

![The 2D design canvas with the results panel and standards scorecard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/madiers/claude-all/claude/blissful-cori-JY3Gi/Content/blog/galtech-design-tools-training/images/2-design-app.png)

## From design to a buildable package

The same project then produces everything the install crew needs. Auto-fill builds a standards-compliant rack by real rules, heavy amplifiers at the bottom, airflow blanks, signal flow top to bottom. The single-line diagram draws every device with its real rear-panel ports, and ends with a numbered connection schedule that lists every port, signal and cable type.

![A numbered connection schedule, ready for the install crew](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/madiers/claude-all/claude/blissful-cori-JY3Gi/Content/blog/galtech-design-tools-training/images/5-wiring-schedule.png)

Video distribution is auto-designed from the rooms you already drew, sizing the smallest HDBaseT matrix that fits or scaling to AV-over-IP for larger, any-to-any jobs. Control and automation sizes an ELAN processor for the touch interfaces on the job, folding in the audio and video you already designed.

![The design rendered as a 3D model with the coverage on the floor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/madiers/claude-all/claude/blissful-cori-JY3Gi/Content/blog/galtech-design-tools-training/images/4-3d-model.png)
