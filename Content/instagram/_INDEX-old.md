# LEA Connect Series — Dante Amps · Instagram carousels

Three design directions for the same post (LEA Professional 4- & 8-channel Dante amplifiers, by Galtech Trading). Each is a self-contained folder — pick one. All are 1080 × 1350 (4:5). Every version includes **feature copy**, a **rear-panel connections slide** with annotated callouts (universal AC · 8× Euroblock analog in · 8× Euroblock speaker out · control + Wi-Fi · redundant Dante Primary + Secondary), and an **"InfoComm 2026 · Best of Show · AV Technology Winner" badge** on the cover (themed per style).

| Folder | Style | Format | Ref it echoes |
|--------|-------|--------|---------------|
| **A — `lea-dante-A-editorial-static`** | Light editorial, elegant Bodoni serif, product on a seamless sweep, leader-line annotations | **Static images** (6 × PNG) | *ESCAPE / mymind* + *Trust the Processor* |
| **B — `lea-dante-B-bold-blue`** | Deep-blue gradient, oversized bold sans overlapping a floating amp, yellow mono kicker + @handle | **Video** (5 × MP4) | *AI Platform UI Design* |
| **C — `lea-dante-C-dark-technical`** | Dark cinematic; real Higgsfield product motion + crisp text; spec-forward | **Video** (6 × MP4) | evolution of the first cut |

Each folder has: numbered post files (upload in order), `_contact_sheet.png` (all slides at a glance), `_cover.jpg` (video sets), `_preview_reel.mp4` (video sets — review only, don't post), and `caption.txt`.

**Recommendation:** **C** for a premium tech feel with motion; **A** if the feed leans editorial/print and you want fast, no-render static posts; **B** for the boldest scroll-stopper.

`lea-dante-carousel/` = the original first cut (no rear-panel/feature slides) — superseded by **C**.
