# Dynamix MK4

**Slug:** dynamix-mk4
**Subtitle:** Wedge-shaped on-wall side/rear surround speaker
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** surround
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/dynamix-mk4

## Description
The Dynamix MK4 is ideal as side or rear surround speakers in a multi-channel setup, designed to complement the Krix range of front speakers. It uses a wedge-shaped design with the same 15-degree angled baffle found in Krix's commercial cinema surround speakers, and a bass-reflex design with a front port to eliminate boomy bass.

**Key features**

- Wedge-shaped on-wall side/rear surround speaker
- 15-degree angled baffle from Krix commercial cinema surrounds
- Bass-reflex design with front port

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 2-way
- **Drivers:** 130mm doped curvilinear paper cone bass/mid + 26mm ring radiator tweeter
- **Frequency response:** 45Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 20-120 Watts RMS
- **Sensitivity:** 88dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms (minimum 3.6 Ohms)
- **Dimensions:** 355mm H x 245mm W x 200mm D (at top)
