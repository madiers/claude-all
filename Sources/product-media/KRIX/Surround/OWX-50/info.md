# OWX-50

**Slug:** owx-50

**Brand:** krix

## Description
The OWX-50 delivers performance and power in a compact design for those needing high output from a small footprint, and can be used as a screen, surround, or overhead speaker. The included bracket allows flush mounting or adjustable angles up to 30 degrees.

Finished in Cinema Black.

**Key features**

- Compact high-output speaker for screen, surround or overhead use
- Dual 165mm drivers with Krix short-throw constant-directivity horn
- Bracket for flush mount or adjustable angles up to 30 degrees

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 2-way bass reflex, front-vented
- **Drivers:** 2 x 165mm (6.5-inch) paper cone, 50mm voice coils + compression driver on constant-directivity horn
- **Mounting:** Adjustable up to 30 degrees
- **Finish:** Cinema Black
