# Phonix Flat

**Slug:** phonix-flat
**Subtitle:** Flat-baffle on-wall surround speaker
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** surround
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/phonix-flat

## Description
A flat-baffle variant of the Phonix designed for on-wall surround positions that sit more on-axis with the listening seats, finished as a slim on-wall cabinet.

**Key features**

- Flat-baffle variant of the Phonix surround
- Slim on-wall cabinet for on-axis placement
- Professional-grade crossover components

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 6.5-inch 2-way
- **Drivers:** 165mm paper cone, 33mm voice coil + 26mm doped fabric dome tweeter
- **Frequency range:** 45Hz - 20kHz (-10dB, in room)
- **Power handling:** 50-150 Watts RMS
- **Sensitivity:** 88dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** 8 Ohms
- **Crossover point:** 1.8kHz
