# Cyclonix 18

**Slug:** cyclonix-18
**Subtitle:** Giant 455mm passive subwoofer for high drive-level applications
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** subwoofer
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/cyclonix-18

## Description
The Cyclonix 18 is a giant passive subwoofer driven by a 455mm bass driver with a 100mm voice coil, powerful vented magnetic circuit and double spider assembly. Its dual front-vented enclosure is tuned for maximum output, with high-pass filter processing for driver protection in high drive-level dedicated cinema applications.

**Key features**

- 455mm bass driver with 100mm voice coil and double spider
- Dual front-vented enclosure tuned for maximum output
- High-pass filter processing at 20Hz, 24dB/octave Butterworth

## Specifications

- **Driver:** 455mm bass driver, 100mm voice coil, vented magnetic circuit, double spider
- **Enclosure:** Dual front-vented
- **Recommended amplifier power:** 200-1400 Watts RMS
- **Protection:** High-pass filter at 20Hz, 24dB/octave Butterworth
