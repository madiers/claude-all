# Cyclonix 15

**Slug:** cyclonix-15
**Subtitle:** High-output passive subwoofer with 380mm driver
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** subwoofer

## Description
The Cyclonix 15 is a high-output passive subwoofer built around a 380mm bass driver with a 100mm edge-wound copper voice coil and dual spider assembly. Its front-firing vent and high-sensitivity driver are tuned for maximum output in demanding dedicated home cinema applications, driven by an external amplifier.

**Key features**

- 380mm bass driver with 100mm edge-wound copper voice coil
- Dual spider assembly and vented magnet for high output
- Front-firing vent tuned for maximum output

## Specifications

- **Driver:** 380mm bass driver, 100mm edge-wound copper voice coil, dual spider, vented magnet
- **Vent:** Front-firing
- **In-room response:** 25Hz - 200Hz
- **Recommended amplifier power:** Up to 1400 Watts RMS
