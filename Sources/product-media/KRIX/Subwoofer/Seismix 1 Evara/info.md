## Description
The Seismix 1 Evara launched alongside the Equinox Evara bookshelf as part of Krix's Evara line, blending the brand's acoustic engineering with a contemporary aesthetic and compact footprint. It is a down-firing active subwoofer designed to deliver deep bass without dominating a room, suited to apartments and smaller living spaces, with a stylish matte finish in multiple colourways.

**Key features**

- Down-firing active subwoofer with compact footprint
- Auto-sensing power, phase and filter adjustment, stereo RCA inputs
- Matte finishes: Studio White, Black, Navy, Green, Charcoal; Australian Made

## Specifications

- **Driver:** 8-inch down-firing, 1.5-inch voice coil, vented magnet
- **Amplifier:** 350 Watts RMS, 700 Watts maximum instantaneous
- **In-room response:** 25Hz - 200Hz
- **Output:** Up to 116dB
- **Controls:** Signal-sensing power, continuously variable 0-180 phase, bypassable low-pass filter
