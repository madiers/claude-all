# Aquatix

**Slug:** aquatix
**Subtitle:** Marine-grade waterproof 5-inch 2-way outdoor speakers
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** outdoor
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/aquatix

## Description
The Aquatix is Krix's waterproof outdoor speaker. It pairs a nominal 130mm polypropylene cone with rubber surround and a 22mm specially damped mylar dome with phase plug. A UV-stabilised polymer enclosure keeps it sun-resistant, while internal ribs reinforce the cabinet and minimise panel resonance.

It is fully weatherproof to IP54 for dust and moisture resistance, with marine-grade stainless steel hardware plus an aluminium grille and mounting bracket to protect against corrosion.

**Key features**

- 130mm polypropylene cone and 22mm damped mylar dome with phase plug
- UV-stabilised polymer enclosure with internal bracing
- IP54 weatherproof with marine-grade stainless hardware

## Specifications

- **LF driver:** Nominal 130mm (5-inch) polypropylene cone, rubber surround
- **HF driver:** 22mm (1-inch) damped mylar dome with phase plug
- **Frequency range:** 70Hz - 20kHz (on-wall response)
- **Power handling:** 20-100 Watts RMS recommended amplifier power
- **Sensitivity:** 87dB for 2.83V at 1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 8 Ohms (minimum 6.7 Ohms)
- **Weatherproofing:** IP54 rated
