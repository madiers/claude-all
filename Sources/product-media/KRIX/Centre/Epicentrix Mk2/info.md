# Epicentrix Mk2

**Slug:** epicentrix-mk2
**Subtitle:** Powerhouse centre channel matched to the Neuphonix Mk2
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** centre
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/epicentrix-mk2

## Description
The Epicentrix Mk2 is a large three-way centre channel that uses identical drivers to the Neuphonix Mk2 floorstander for a seamless front-stage match. With four 130mm bass drivers, a 130mm midrange and a dual-concentric tweeter, it delivers dialogue, effects and music with finesse and authority.

It was named Centre Speaker of the Year.

**Key features**

- Three-way centre using the same drivers as the Neuphonix Mk2
- Four 130mm bass drivers, 130mm midrange, dual-concentric tweeter
- Named Centre Speaker of the Year

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 3-way
- **Drivers:** 4 x 130mm bass + 130mm midrange + 26mm dual-concentric tweeter
- **Frequency response:** 35Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 50-300 Watts RMS recommended
- **Sensitivity:** 90dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms (minimum 4.3 Ohms)
- **Dimensions:** 233mm H x 910mm W x 355mm D
- **Weight:** 24kg
