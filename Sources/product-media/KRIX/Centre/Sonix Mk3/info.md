# Sonix Mk3

**Slug:** sonix-mk3
**Subtitle:** Slim, compact 2-way D'Appolito entry-level centre channel
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** centre
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/sonix-mk3

## Description
The Sonix Mk3 is Krix's slim, compact entry centre channel, using dual 100mm doped paper-cone drivers in a D'Appolito layout with a 26mm dual-concentric waveguide tweeter.

Its low-profile bass-reflex (front-vented) enclosure with internal X-bracing suits placement under or in front of a TV.

**Key features**

- Slim, compact entry-level centre channel
- Dual 100mm doped paper-cone drivers in D'Appolito layout
- Low-profile front-vented enclosure with internal X-bracing

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** D'Appolito, 2-way
- **Drivers:** 2 x 100mm (4-inch) doped paper cone + 26mm dual-concentric tweeter
- **Enclosure:** Bass reflex, front vented with internal X-bracing
- **Frequency response:** 65Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 20-140 Watts RMS recommended
- **Sensitivity:** 88dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms (minimum 4.2 Ohms)
- **Dimensions:** 125mm H x 435mm W x 160mm D
- **Weight:** 4kg
