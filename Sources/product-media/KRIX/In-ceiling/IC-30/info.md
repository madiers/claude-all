## Description
The IC-30 (Hemispherix AS) is part of Krix's Architectural Series, suitable for home theatre, stereo listening, or high-quality background music. It uses a 100mm driver with large magnet and aluminium shorting ring for accurate bass and midrange clarity, plus a 26mm ring radiator tweeter that swivels to direct sound toward the listener.

A fully-enclosed back box maximises and controls bass, protects from ceiling debris, and drastically reduces sound leakage into adjoining rooms. It is tonally balanced to the Krix floorstanding and bookshelf range.

**Key features**

- 100mm driver with large magnet and aluminium shorting ring
- 26mm swivel ring radiator tweeter aims sound at the listener
- Fully-enclosed back box controls bass and reduces sound leakage
