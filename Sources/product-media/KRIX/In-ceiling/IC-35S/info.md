## Description
The IC-35S (Hemispherix SPS) is a single-point stereo speaker with dual tweeters and a dual voice coil woofer, accepting both left and right channels for high-fidelity music playback from just one speaker. It uses a 5-inch woofer and two 20mm ring radiator tweeters with a low-profile 90mm mounting depth.

It is IP54 rated and suitable for indoor and outdoor use, and the slim, paintable bezel grille locks onto magnets for easy installation.

**Key features**

- Single-point stereo: left and right channels from one speaker
- 5-inch dual voice coil woofer with two 20mm ring radiator tweeters
- IP54 rated for indoor and outdoor use; 90mm mounting depth

## Specifications

- **Woofer:** 5-inch (130mm) dual voice coil
- **Tweeters:** 2 x 20mm ring radiator
- **Mounting depth:** 90mm
- **Weatherproofing:** IP54 rated
