## Description
The IC-20 (Helix) features a large 150mm bass driver with oversized magnet and a 20mm dome tweeter in a dual-concentric configuration that gives uniform sound coverage in all directions. It uses weather-resistant materials and a rust-resistant stainless steel grille in a semi-enclosed design, with a low-profile 86mm mounting depth.

A twist-and-lock mounting system makes installation quick, and it is suitable for both indoor and outdoor use.

**Key features**

- Dual-concentric 150mm bass driver and 20mm dome tweeter
- Weather-resistant build with rust-resistant stainless steel grille
- Low-profile 86mm mounting depth with twist-and-lock install

## Specifications

- **Drivers:** 150mm bass + 20mm dome tweeter (dual concentric)
- **Power handling:** 10-120 Watts RMS
- **Impedance:** Nominally 8 Ohms (minimum 6 Ohms)
- **Sensitivity:** 86dB for 2.83V at 1m
- **Mounting depth:** 86mm
- **Cut-out diameter:** 164mm
