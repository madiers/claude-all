## Description
The Esoterix Altum is Krix's premium 2-way bookshelf speaker aimed at audiophile 2-channel listening. It pairs a 165mm bass/midrange driver (cast aluminium basket, large neodymium-iron-boron magnet) with a ScanSpeak Revelator tweeter for an accurate, detailed stereo image.

A precision waveguide controls directivity to present a soundstage larger than the cabinet size suggests, with a tight, punchy low end.

**Key features**

- Premium 2-way audiophile bookshelf speaker
- 165mm bass/midrange with cast aluminium basket and NdFeB magnet
- ScanSpeak Revelator tweeter with precision directivity waveguide

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 2-way
- **Bass/midrange:** 165mm (cast aluminium basket, NdFeB magnet)
- **Tweeter:** ScanSpeak Revelator
- **Frequency response:** 40Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 20-200 Watts RMS recommended
- **Sensitivity:** 89dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 4 Ohms (minimum 3.2 Ohms)
- **Dimensions:** 390mm H x 260mm W x 380mm D
