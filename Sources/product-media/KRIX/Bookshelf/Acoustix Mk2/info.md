# Acoustix Mk2

**Slug:** acoustix-mk2
**Subtitle:** Larger bookshelf speaker with dual 5-inch drivers
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** bookshelf
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/acoustix-mk2

## Description
The Acoustix Mk2 is a bookshelf speaker using dual 130mm (5-inch) bass/mid drivers and a 26mm dual-concentric tweeter with waveguide centre plug for controlled directivity.

It is the largest of Krix's compact bookshelf trio, timbre-matched to the rest of the range.

**Key features**

- Dual 130mm bass/mid drivers
- 26mm dual-concentric tweeter with waveguide centre plug
- Largest of the Krix compact bookshelf trio, timbre-matched to the range

## Specifications

- **Drivers:** 2 x 130mm (5-inch) bass/mid + 26mm dual-concentric tweeter
- **Frequency response:** 45Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 20-160 Watts RMS recommended
- **Sensitivity:** 89dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms (minimum 4.3 Ohms)
- **Dimensions:** 450mm H x 180mm W x 300mm D
- **Weight:** 10kg each
