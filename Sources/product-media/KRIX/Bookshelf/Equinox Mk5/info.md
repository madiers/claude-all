# Equinox Mk5

**Slug:** equinox-mk5

**Category:** bookshelf

## Description
The Equinox Mk5 is a compact bass-reflex bookshelf speaker built around a 130mm doped curvilinear paper-cone bass/mid driver and a 26mm ring-radiator tweeter.

A long-standing favourite among 2-channel enthusiasts, it can also serve as a surround speaker in a multi-channel Krix system when used on stands or mounts.

**Key features**

- Compact bass-reflex bookshelf speaker
- 130mm doped curvilinear paper-cone bass/mid driver
- 26mm ring-radiator tweeter; doubles as a surround speaker

## Specifications

- **Drivers:** 130mm doped curvilinear paper-cone bass/mid + 26mm ring radiator
- **Enclosure:** Bass reflex, rear vented
- **Frequency response:** 45Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 20-120 Watts RMS recommended
- **Sensitivity:** 88dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms (minimum 3.6 Ohms)
- **Dimensions:** 295mm H x 175mm W x 235mm D
- **Weight:** 6kg
