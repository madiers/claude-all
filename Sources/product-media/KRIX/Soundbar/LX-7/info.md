# LX-7

**Slug:** lx-7
**Subtitle:** Passive Linear LCR bar for screens 75 inches and above
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** soundbar
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/lx-7

## Description
The LX-7 is a high-performance passive loudspeaker designed and built by Krix in South Australia, intended for TVs and screens of 75 inches and above. It combines left, centre and right channels in one bar and has no internal amplifiers - you supply three channels of amplification (typically from a multichannel AV receiver), avoiding the limitations of the low-powered amps in conventional soundbars.

It is intended to be paired with Krix subwoofer(s) for the lowest frequencies and is tonally matched to all Krix surround and overhead speakers. Available in standard and custom lengths.

**Key features**

- Three discrete L/C/R channels in one passive bar
- No built-in amps - driven by your AV receiver or power amp
- Tonally matched to Krix surround and overhead speakers; custom sizes available

## Specifications

- **Bass driver (per channel):** 165mm (6.5-inch) paper cone, 50mm voice coil
- **Tweeter (per channel):** 26mm ferrofluid-cooled doped-fabric dome in 90x90 waveguide
- **Crossover:** 1.9kHz
- **Frequency response:** 60Hz - 20kHz (in room)
- **Impedance:** Nominally 8 Ohms per channel
- **Sensitivity:** 92dB (2.83V/1m)
- **Recommended input power:** 50-250 Watts RMS
- **Dimensions:** 220mm H x 142mm D; lengths 1670mm (75-inch) to 2260mm (custom)
