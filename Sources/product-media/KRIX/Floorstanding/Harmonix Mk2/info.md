# Harmonix Mk2

**Slug:** harmonix-mk2
**Subtitle:** Three-way floorstander with rich detail, warm tone and deep bass
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** floorstanding
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/harmonix-mk2

## Description
The Harmonix Mk2 is a three-way floorstander using dual 165mm laminated polypropylene bass drivers in a dual-chamber enclosure (sealed midrange chamber with a bass-reflex main chamber). Front-facing ports let it sit close to a wall without compromising sound.

It is timbre-matched to the rest of the Krix range for seamless surround fidelity.

**Key features**

- Three-way design with dual 165mm laminated polypropylene bass drivers
- Dual-chamber enclosure with front-facing ports for near-wall placement
- Timbre-matched to the rest of the Krix range

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 3-way
- **Bass drivers:** 2 x 165mm (6.5-inch) laminated polypropylene cone
- **Frequency response:** 35Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 50-200 Watts RMS
- **Sensitivity:** 90dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms (minimum 3.1 Ohms)
- **Dimensions:** 1040mm H x 220mm W x 350mm D
