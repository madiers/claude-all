## Description
### Robust whole-home control at a price point that opens new opportunities.

The Nice EL-SC-150 System Controller offers robust performance at a price point that creates new opportunities for smaller to moderate sized installations, providing a single point of control and automation for media, climate, lighting, security, surveillance, and more. All control is conducted via the award-winning Nice smart home management user interface for an exceptional experience.

- ARM big.LITTLE architecture integrating dual-core and quad-core processors
- HDMI 2.0 for 4K 60Hz output with HDCP 1.4/2.2 support
- H.265 video decoding capability up to 4K resolution

**Flexible, reliable design**

- Ultra-efficient, fanless design with no moving parts for long-lasting reliability
- PoE or Wi-Fi connection for placement flexibility
- Compact enclosure fits in cabinets, structured wiring cabinets, or behind a display

Pair with the Nice EL-IO-200 as a cost-effective way of adding control connections to another area.
