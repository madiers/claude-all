## Description
### Dynamic, impactful sound in a compact, design-friendly form factor.

The SC-IC3 High Performance 3.5" In-Ceiling Speaker delivers dynamic and impactful sound in a compact package. Leveraging advanced technology and materials, its high-excursion carbon fiber woofer, proportionately massive aluminum tweeter, and custom crossover design make it a top choice for premium performance without compromising aesthetics. Both round and square grilles are included, with optional flush mount kits, to satisfy the most fastidious designer's needs.

- 3.5" carbon fiber woofer: stiff, light, and strong for transparent, low-distortion sound
- 1" aluminum tweeter, proportionately massive for dynamic detail at a lower crossover point
- Custom crossover with proprietary Capacitor Filter/Boost Circuitry for punchy, rich bass
- 4.75" cut-out size uses a standard hole saw for quick retrofit installs
- Optional flush mount mudbank for a near-invisible aesthetic (sold separately)

**Designed as a system**

- Engineered in tandem with the SC-IC-SUB8 Small Aperture in-ceiling subwoofer
- Includes two low-profile, paintable round and square grilles
