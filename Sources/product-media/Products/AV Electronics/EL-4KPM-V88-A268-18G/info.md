## Description

### An 8x8 HDBaseT matrix with independent 26x8 audio matrix and ARC.

The Nice EL-4KPM-V88-A268-18G is an HDMI 2.0 4K 60Hz 4:4:4, HDCP 2.2 matrix that uses CSC technology to deliver HDMI, bi-directional IR and PoC over a single CAT cable. Built for the custom installation market, it adds simultaneous HDBaseT/HDMI on output 1, video down-conversion on HDBaseT outputs and an independent 26x8 audio matrix including Audio Return Channel (ARC).

- Eight HDMI inputs independently routable to eight HDBaseT outputs
- Output 1 features simultaneous HDMI and HDBaseT output
- Built-in web browser interface for control and configuration, with RS-232 and IR pass-through for 3rd party control

**Distribution and audio**

- Supports 4K 60Hz 4:4:4 UHD video up to 70m, and 1080p video up to 100m
- Video down-conversion on HDBaseT outputs so lower-resolution displays still receive content while other outputs keep full 4K UHD
- Independent 26x8 audio matrix: 8 audio breakouts from HDMI inputs, 8 from zone outputs, 8 ARC from zone outputs, plus 1 optical and 1 analog audio input
- ARC feature compatible with the EL-4KHDBT-RX-70-ARC-18G receiver

**Control and power**

- Control via front panel, IR, RS-232 and TCP/IP, with bi-directional IR and RS-232 on all HDBaseT outputs
- Supports PoC to power compatible HDBaseT receivers; supplied with Nice 5V 3.5mm IR receivers and emitters
- Advanced EDID management and HDCP 2.2 compliant

A 2U rack-mountable chassis with included rack ears, supplied with a remote control, IR emitters/receivers, serial and IR control cables, and IEC power cable(s). A CR2025 battery is required for the remote control (not included).
