## Description
### Send audio over a standard network from a single source.

The Nice EL-IPD-PRE-SIO Network Audio Interface enables the delivery of bidirectional audio over a standard local area network. It sends audio from a single remote source to a Nice-enabled amplifier or preamplifier, or to a second amplifier, using Dante audio networking. Nice IP-enabled audio distribution simplifies whole-house audio and enhances the intelligent home by delivering high-quality audio over an existing network.

- Three selectable inputs: optical TOSLINK, coaxial RCA, or analog RCA
- Pre-amp outputs: analog RCA plus dedicated subwoofer RCA
- Bidirectional audio networking using Dante

**Power and connectivity**

- Powered over PoE (802.3af / 802.3at Type 1) or DC 5V, 2A
- 100 Mbps Ethernet with DHCP enabled by default
- Compact, lightweight chassis at just 0.65 lbs

Easily scaling from a single chassis solution to a decentralized multi-chassis, multi-source implementation, the EL-IPD-PRE-SIO meets the needs of projects of any size.
