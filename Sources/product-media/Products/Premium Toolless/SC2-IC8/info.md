## Description
### Bigger sound for immersive entertainment.

The Nice SC2-IC8 is a Series 2 premium toolless in-ceiling speaker that steps up to an 8 inch woofer for greater output and deeper low-end, ideal for immersive gaming, streaming, and distributed audio in larger spaces. Anodized ceramic/aluminum drivers deliver crisp, dynamic, crystal clear sound.

- 8 inch anodized ceramic/aluminum woofer for room-filling output
- 1 1/4 inch (30mm) anodized ceramic/aluminum dome tweeter lowers the crossover point for exceptional imagery
- XMag longer pole piece ensures precise linearity and accurate reproduction

**ViseLock toolless installation**

- ViseLock mounting system designed by Swarm enables installs up to 75% faster than conventional systems
- Electroplated painted steel grill resists rust and is paintable to match any ceiling

Compatible with the optional FRBC-UNI 1-hour fire-rated cover. Backed by a 5 year warranty.
