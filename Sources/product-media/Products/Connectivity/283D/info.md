## Description
### Single IR emitter that confirms every command with a visible red flash.

The 283D Blink-IR Designer Emitter houses a small infrared LED in a miniature, designer-styled, black injection-molded plastic shell. Unlike standard emitters, the 283D emits visible red light in addition to the infrared control signals when activated by IR commands from IR receivers or other controllers. It is designed to mount directly on the IR sensor window of the controlled device.

- Emits visible red light alongside IR signals for at-a-glance command confirmation
- Clear adhesive layer on the flat base for direct attachment to a component's IR sensor window
- IR-transparent shell allows handheld remote commands to pass straight through it

**Key details**

- 3.5mm mono mini plug, approximately 10 ft cable length
- White-striped side of the plug is positive (+)
- Connects to the EMITTERS or OUT jacks on connecting blocks and controllers

The 283D also flags stray IR or RF interference by randomly blinking when no control signal is being sent, helping installers reposition interfering sources for a cleaner system. An optional 28DES shield is available to block external IR for zone-controlled applications.
