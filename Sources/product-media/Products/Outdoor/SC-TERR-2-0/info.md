## Description
### Impressive audio quality from patio to poolside.

The Nice Terrazza SC-TERR-2.0 kit adds two weatherproof 4" Terrazza satellite speakers to expand your outdoor system. Designed to grow a Terrazza installation up to an 8.2 configuration, it uses the same value-priced, over-delivering Terrazza satellites that pair with lower-powered distributed audio amplifiers or extended AVR zones — no expensive high-power amplifier required.

- Two 4" weatherproof satellites with polypropylene cone woofer and 3/4" titanium dome tweeter
- Up to 100W (max) power handling
- Frequency response 120Hz-20kHz

**Easy, toolless installation**

- Quick-connect satellite design for thread-free attachment, with post-install rotation and tilt via thumbscrew
- Handy hammer cap protects the stake while driving it into the ground
- Silicone-filled wire connectors included for all connections

The simplest way to add coverage to a Terrazza system or build out toward an 8.2 configuration.
