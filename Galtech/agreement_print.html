1 — Appointment

1.1

Galtech appoints the Dealer as an authorised ELAN dealer for the territory stated on page 1. The appointment is not exclusive.

1.2

The Dealer sells only to end-user customers as part of a professionally installed system. No resale to other dealers, traders or online marketplaces, and no export outside the territory.

2 — Certification conditions

Both conditions are mandatory
2.1

Training. At least one employee of the Dealer must attend and pass the full three-day ELAN hands-on training delivered by Galtech. Attendance on all three days is compulsory. Certificates are issued in the personal name of the attendee.

2.2

Demo kit. The Dealer must purchase the ELAN demo system at AED 17,000, pay for it in full, and keep it installed, updated and fully operational at its showroom or office for live demonstration. The demo system comprises:

2.3

Training seats included. The demo system purchase includes two training seats, valid for two years from the invoice date, for employees of the Dealer only. They cover the three-day training and the annual refresher session held in that period.

2.4

Certified Dealer status is granted in writing by Galtech only after both conditions above are met. Until then, the Dealer may not purchase ELAN controllers or licences, be listed as an ELAN dealer, or use the ELAN or Nice name or logo.

3 — Partner levels

3.1

A certified Dealer holds one of the three partner levels ticked on page 1. Levels are awarded on the purchase volume of the preceding twelve months and reviewed at each annual renewal.

LevelAnnual volumeStatus and benefits
Selected Certified status, dealer pricing, technical and commissioning support, and use of the Selected Partner badge.
Preferred All Selected benefits, plus project pricing, priority technical support and listing as an authorised ELAN dealer.
Prestige All Preferred benefits, plus best project pricing, priority on lead referral, marketing and demonstration support.
3.2

The selected badge may be used only in the form supplied by Galtech and while Certification is valid.

4 — Validity and renewal

4.1

Certification is valid for twelve months and renews annually if the Dealer attends the annual refresher training, keeps the demo kit operational and updated, and has no overdue invoices.

4.2

Galtech may suspend or withdraw Certification if the certified technician leaves and is not replaced within ninety days, if the demo kit is removed or not working, if renewal is not completed, or if payments are overdue.

5 — Dealer obligations

5.1

Every ELAN system is designed, installed, programmed and commissioned by or under the supervision of a certified technician of the Dealer.

5.2

The Dealer is responsible for the support of the systems it has installed for its customers, including first-line support, service and after-sales assistance, and registers each installation with Galtech.

5.3

The ELAN and Nice names and logos remain the property of the manufacturer and may be used only while Certification is valid and only in the approved form. Dealer prices are confidential.

6 — Commercial and general

6.1

Products are supplied at the Galtech dealer price in force at the date of order. Payment in advance unless written credit terms are agreed. Title passes on full payment.

6.2

Products carry the standard manufacturer warranty administered by Galtech. It does not cover incorrect installation or programming, or work by a person who is not certified. Labour and site costs are for the Dealer.

6.3

Either party may terminate on ninety days’ written notice. Galtech may terminate immediately if Certification is withdrawn, if clause 1.2 is breached, or if payment is overdue by more than thirty days.

Execution

Signed in agreement of the above, in particular the certification conditions in clause 2.
