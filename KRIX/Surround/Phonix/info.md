# Phonix

**Slug:** phonix
**Subtitle:** Benchmark on-wall side/rear surround loudspeaker
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** surround
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/phonix

## Description
Designed for on-wall side and rear surround sound, the Phonix offers high power handling with low-frequency extension down to 45Hz. It features a braced, critically-damped enclosure finished with a neat black cloth grille.

It replicates the wedge-shaped design used in Krix's commercial cinema surround speakers so it can be mounted high and away from the listener while maintaining pattern control.

**Key features**

- On-wall side and rear surround loudspeaker
- Braced, critically-damped enclosure with black cloth grille
- Wedge-shaped cinema-derived design for pattern control

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 6.5-inch 2-way
- **Drivers:** 165mm bass driver, 33mm voice coil + 26mm doped fabric dome tweeter
- **Frequency range:** 45Hz - 20kHz (-10dB)
- **Power handling:** 50-150 Watts RMS
- **Sensitivity:** 88dB SPL, 2.83V at 1m
- **Impedance:** 8 Ohms
- **Crossover point:** 1.8kHz
