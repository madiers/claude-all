# Phonix 45

**Slug:** phonix-45
**Subtitle:** Shallow-depth angled surround / ceiling-effect speaker
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** surround
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/phonix-45

## Description
The Phonix 45 has a shallow 124mm depth and provides an immersive home theatre experience as a surround or ceiling-effect speaker. It shares the Phonix driver complement in a compact angled-baffle cabinet.

**Key features**

- Shallow 124mm depth angled-baffle cabinet
- Works as a surround or ceiling-effect speaker
- Shares the Phonix driver complement

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 6.5-inch 2-way
- **Drivers:** 165mm laminated polypropylene cone, 33mm voice coil + 26mm doped fabric dome
- **Frequency response:** 45Hz - 20kHz (-10dB)
- **Power handling:** 50-150 Watts RMS
- **Sensitivity:** 88dB SPL, 2.83V at 1m
- **Impedance:** 8 Ohms
- **Crossover point:** 1.8kHz
- **Depth:** 124mm
