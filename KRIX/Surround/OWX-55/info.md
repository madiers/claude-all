# OWX-55

**Slug:** owx-55
**Subtitle:** Vertical-orientation compact high-output on-wall speaker
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** surround
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/owx-55

## Description
The OWX-55 shares the OWX-50's output and driver design but in a taller, vertical orientation, and can be used as a screen, surround, or overhead speaker. Its vertical design allows adjustable mounting angles up to 68 degrees.

Finished in Cinema Black.

**Key features**

- Vertical-orientation compact high-output speaker
- Dual 165mm drivers with Krix constant-directivity horn
- Adjustable mounting angles up to 68 degrees

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 2-way bass reflex, front-vented
- **Drivers:** 2 x 165mm (6.5-inch) paper cone, 50mm voice coils + high-output compression driver on horn
- **Mounting:** Adjustable up to 68 degrees
- **Finish:** Cinema Black
