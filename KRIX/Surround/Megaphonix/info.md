# Megaphonix

**Slug:** megaphonix
**Subtitle:** High-output on-wall cinema main/surround speaker
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** surround
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/megaphonix

## Description
Designed for on-wall use in dedicated home cinemas, the Megaphonix uses high power-handling and high-sensitivity drivers to deliver cinema-grade impact and dynamics without needing a high-wattage amplifier. It is braced and critically damped for improved bass extension.

It is part of a five-speaker family that can be combined with matching mains and surrounds.

**Key features**

- High-output 10-inch on-wall cinema speaker
- High sensitivity for cinema impact without a big amplifier
- Braced, critically damped enclosure; part of the Megaphonix family

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 10-inch 2-way
- **Drivers:** 255mm bass driver, 50mm voice coil + 26mm doped fabric dome with proprietary waveguide
- **Dispersion:** 90 degrees horizontal / 40 degrees vertical
- **Frequency range:** 40Hz - 20kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 50-250 Watts RMS
- **Sensitivity:** 95dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** 8 Ohms
