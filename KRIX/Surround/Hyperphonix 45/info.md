# Hyperphonix 45

**Slug:** hyperphonix-45
**Subtitle:** High-output 45-degree surround and overhead speaker
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** surround
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/hyperphonix-45

## Description
The Hyperphonix 45 is a high-power, high-sensitivity surround and overhead speaker featuring a 45-degree baffle that aims sound directly at the listening seat. It uses a newly developed 90 x 90 Krix short-throw waveguide to improve dispersion and efficiency for very high output, and can be mounted flat to the wall or tightly into a corner.

It supports immersive formats like Dolby Atmos and DTS:X and ships as a pair with an adjustable bracket and black cloth grille.

**Key features**

- 45-degree baffle aims sound at the listening seat
- 90 x 90 Krix short-throw waveguide for high output
- Supports Dolby Atmos and DTS:X; ships with adjustable bracket

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 2-way
- **Drivers:** 165mm paper cone with 50mm voice coil + 26mm doped fabric dome on 90x90 waveguide
- **Frequency response:** 60Hz - 20kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 50-250 Watts RMS
- **Sensitivity:** 92dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** 8 Ohms
