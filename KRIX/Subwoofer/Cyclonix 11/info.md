# Cyclonix 11

**Slug:** cyclonix-11
**Subtitle:** Low-profile passive subwoofer with 275mm driver
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** subwoofer
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/cyclonix-11

## Description
The Cyclonix 11 is a low-profile passive subwoofer requiring an external amplifier (connected via the LFE output of an AV processor or receiver). Its shallow-depth design can be positioned against walls or behind acoustically transparent screens, working as a support sub or standalone within a dedicated home cinema setup.

**Key features**

- Passive design driven by an external amplifier
- Shallow-depth cabinet for flexible placement
- Single down-firing vent for deep, controlled bass

## Specifications

- **Driver:** 275mm bass driver, 50mm voice coil, ventilated magnet
- **Vent:** Single down-firing
- **Recommended amplifier power:** 200-350 Watts RMS
- **In-room response:** 30Hz - 200Hz
- **Sensitivity:** 90dB SPL (2.83V/1m)
