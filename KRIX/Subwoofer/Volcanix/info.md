# Volcanix

**Slug:** volcanix
**Subtitle:** The peak of home theatre bass delivery
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** subwoofer
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/volcanix

## Description
The Volcanix is positioned as the pinnacle of Krix's home-theatre bass range. A massively powerful 305mm long-throw bass driver is at its heart, developed for high-level, low-frequency reinforcement. Krix's own amplifier design uses ICEpower class D technology for improved efficiency and lower power consumption.

Simple controls and a back-lit LCD display (which fades after 45 seconds) make set-up easy, and it is offered in classic black woodgrain or five genuine timber veneer finishes.

**Key features**

- 305mm long-throw bass driver with 50mm voice coil
- Krix-designed ICEpower class D amplifier
- Back-lit LCD control display; black woodgrain or timber veneer finishes

## Specifications

- **Low-frequency driver:** Nominal 305mm (12-inch) paper cone, 50mm (2-inch) long throw voice coil
- **Amplifier power:** 450 Watts RMS, 900 Watts maximum instantaneous
- **In-room response:** 15Hz - 200Hz
- **Output:** 125dB maximum SPL (in room)
- **Enclosure:** Bass reflex, front vented
- **Dimensions:** 506mm H x 400mm W x 470mm D
