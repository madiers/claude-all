# Cyclonix 12

**Slug:** cyclonix-12
**Subtitle:** Low-profile passive subwoofer with 305mm driver and dual down-firing vents
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** subwoofer
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/cyclonix-12

## Description
The Cyclonix 12 is a low-profile passive subwoofer with a 305mm long-throw driver and dual down-firing vents tuned for maximum output. Requiring an external amplifier, its shallow-depth design can be placed against walls or behind acoustically transparent screens for dedicated home cinema applications.

**Key features**

- 305mm long-throw driver with dual down-firing vents
- Passive design driven by an external amplifier
- Low-profile cabinet for flexible placement

## Specifications

- **Driver:** 305mm (12-inch) paper cone, 50mm long throw voice coil, ventilated magnet
- **Vents:** Dual down-firing
- **Recommended amplifier power:** 200-500 Watts RMS
- **In-room response:** 25Hz - 200Hz
- **Sensitivity:** 92dB SPL (2.83V/1m)
