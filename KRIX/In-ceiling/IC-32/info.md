## Description
The IC-32 (Hemispherix A20) delivers midrange clarity from a 100mm driver with large magnet and aluminium shorting ring, with extended highs from a 26mm ring radiator tweeter. It uses a 20-degree angled baffle to project sound toward the primary listening area, reducing reflections off walls and surfaces.

A fully-enclosed back box controls bass and reduces sound leakage, and the slim paintable rust-resistant stainless steel bezel grille locks onto magnets for easy installation.

**Key features**

- 100mm driver and 26mm ring radiator tweeter
- 20-degree angled baffle aims sound at the listening area
- Fully-enclosed back box with slim, paintable magnetic grille

## Specifications

- **Drivers:** 100mm driver + 26mm ring radiator tweeter
- **Frequency response:** 65Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Baffle:** 20-degree angled
