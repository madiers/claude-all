## Description
The IC-10 (Holographix) is a downlight-sized speaker that hides in the ceiling to distribute music seamlessly and invisibly. Despite its small size it delivers high quality performance thanks to a full-range 75mm polypropylene cone with a large voice coil and magnet.

A simple twist-and-lock mounting system makes installation easy, multiple pairs can be wired together for even coverage over large areas, and it can be painted to blend into any colour scheme. It is semi-enclosed, protected from dust and ceiling debris, and water resistant.

**Key features**

- Full-range 75mm polypropylene cone with large voice coil and magnet
- Twist-and-lock mounting for fast, easy installation
- Paintable grille and semi-enclosed, water-resistant design

## Specifications

- **Driver:** Full range 75mm polypropylene cone
- **Power handling:** 10-80 Watts RMS recommended amplifier power
- **Impedance:** Nominally 8 Ohms (minimum 7.5 Ohms)
- **Frequency response:** 90Hz - 20kHz
- **Mounting depth:** 108mm
- **Dimensions:** 95mm diameter x 108mm
