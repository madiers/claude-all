## Description
The IC-80 (Stratospherix AS) is as at home in an outdoor entertaining area as in a home theatre. It is driven by a massive 200mm bass driver with oversized magnet and a professional-grade compression tweeter in a dual-concentric configuration for uniform coverage in all directions.

A semi-enclosed back box ensures consistent, extended bass and protects against dust and debris, while the slim-bezel rust-resistant stainless steel grille and weatherproof build handle all conditions. It also suits high-volume indoor uses such as large concealed home cinemas.

**Key features**

- 200mm bass driver with oversized magnet
- Professional-grade compression tweeter in dual-concentric layout
- Weatherproof build with semi-enclosed back box and slim grille

## Specifications

- **Bass driver:** 200mm (oversized magnet)
- **Tweeter:** Professional-grade compression (dual concentric)
- **Frequency response:** 45Hz - 20kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 20-200 Watts RMS
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms
- **Sensitivity:** 93dB (2.83V at 1m)
