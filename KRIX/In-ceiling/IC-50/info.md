## Description
The IC-50 (Atmospherix AS) is a discreet in-ceiling speaker that performs well for stereo music listening and high-quality background music across multiple rooms. It features a large 130mm bass driver with large magnet and copper shorting ring for bass and midrange clarity, and a 26mm ring radiator for extended high frequencies, with the components mounted directly on the front baffle (like Krix bookshelf speakers) for true hi-fi performance.

The rust-resistant stainless steel grille snaps into place with magnets, and a fully-enclosed back box protects the components from dust and debris while optimising bass and reducing sound leakage to other rooms.

**Key features**

- 130mm bass driver with large magnet and copper shorting ring
- 26mm ring radiator tweeter for extended highs
- Baffle-mounted components and fully-enclosed back box for hi-fi sound

## Specifications

- **Bass driver:** 130mm (large magnet, copper shorting ring)
- **Tweeter:** 26mm ring radiator
- **Enclosure:** Fully-enclosed back box
