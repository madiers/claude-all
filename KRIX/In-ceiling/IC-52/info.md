# IC-52

## Description
The IC-52 (Atmospherix A20) features a 130mm bass driver with large magnet and copper shorting ring for bass and midrange clarity, plus a 26mm ring radiator for high frequencies, with the bass driver and tweeter separately mounted on the front baffle (like Krix bookshelf speakers) for true hi-fi performance. A 20-degree angled baffle behind the slim magnetic grille projects sound toward the listening position for improved clarity in music and movies.

A fully-enclosed back box optimises bass in any setting and protects from ceiling debris and sound leakage. Designed, engineered, assembled and tested in Australia.

**Key features**

- 130mm bass driver with copper shorting ring and 26mm ring radiator
- 20-degree angled baffle aims sound at the listening position
- Fully-enclosed back box; slim paintable stainless steel grille

## Specifications

- **Bass driver:** 130mm (large magnet, copper shorting ring)
- **Tweeter:** 26mm ring radiator
- **Baffle:** 20-degree angled
- **Enclosure:** Fully-enclosed back box
