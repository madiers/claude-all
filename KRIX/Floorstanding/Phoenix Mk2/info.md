# Phoenix Mk2

**Slug:** phoenix-mk2
**Subtitle:** Compact D'Appolito 2-way floorstander
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** floorstanding
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/phoenix-mk2

## Description
The Phoenix Mk2 is a slim 2-way D'Appolito floorstander (under 20cm wide) built around dual 165mm laminated polypropylene drivers with 33mm voice coils, flanking a dual-concentric tweeter.

It is positioned as the ideal home-entertainment floorstander for multi-channel or stereo listening where floor space is limited.

**Key features**

- Slim D'Appolito 2-way floorstander under 20cm wide
- Dual 165mm laminated polypropylene drivers with 33mm voice coils
- Dual-concentric tweeter with waveguide centre plug

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** D'Appolito, 2-way
- **Bass drivers:** 2 x 165mm (6.5-inch) laminated polypropylene cone
- **Tweeter:** 26mm dual-concentric with waveguide centre plug
- **Frequency response:** 35Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 50-200 Watts RMS recommended
- **Sensitivity:** 91dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms (minimum 3.5 Ohms)
- **Dimensions:** 950mm H x 195mm W x 295mm D
