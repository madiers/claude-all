# Tropix

**Slug:** tropix
**Subtitle:** All-weather outdoor speakers with big sound
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** outdoor
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/tropix

## Description
The all-weather Tropix is built to elevate outdoor entertaining with seriously big sound, offering smooth response and deep extension for high-quality music outdoors. It uses a 165mm bass driver and a 26mm damped mylar dome tweeter for high sensitivity and extended low frequencies.

The enclosure is marine-grade, all-weather and UV-stabilised, with internal ribs that reinforce the cabinet, minimise panel resonance and maximise bass. Marine-grade stainless hardware plus an aluminium grille and mounting bracket protect against corrosion.

**Key features**

- 165mm bass driver with 26mm damped mylar dome tweeter
- Marine-grade, UV-stabilised all-weather enclosure
- Marine-grade stainless hardware and aluminium grille and bracket

## Specifications

- **Bass driver:** 165mm (6.5-inch)
- **Tweeter:** 26mm damped mylar dome
- **Power handling:** 20-140 Watts RMS recommended amplifier power
- **Sensitivity:** 87dB for 2.83V at 1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 8 Ohms
