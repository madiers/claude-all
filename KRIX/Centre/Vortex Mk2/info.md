# Vortex Mk2

**Slug:** vortex-mk2
**Subtitle:** Flagship 3-way centre with vocal clarity and dynamic bass
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** centre
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/vortex-mk2

## Description
The Vortex Mk2 is Krix's large three-way centre channel, with dual 165mm bass drivers, a dedicated 130mm midrange, and a dual-concentric tweeter in a dual-chamber enclosure.

Krix positions it as the ideal partner to the Harmonix Mk2 for the ultimate in realism in a home-theatre front stage.

**Key features**

- Large three-way centre channel
- Dual 165mm bass drivers, 130mm midrange, dual-concentric tweeter
- Dual-chamber enclosure; partners the Harmonix Mk2 floorstander

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 3-way
- **Drivers:** 2 x 165mm bass + 130mm midrange + 25mm dual-concentric tweeter
- **Frequency response:** 35Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 50-250 Watts RMS recommended
- **Sensitivity:** 91dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms (minimum 3 Ohms)
- **Dimensions:** 230mm H x 725mm W x 370mm D
- **Weight:** 24kg each
