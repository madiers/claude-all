# Graphix Mk2

**Slug:** graphix-mk2

## Description
The Graphix Mk2 is a compact D'Appolito two-way centre with dual 130mm bass/mid drivers flanking a 26mm waveguide tweeter. Dual front-firing ports let it be placed inside AV furniture or cabinets without boomy bass resonance, making it a flexible centre option for space-constrained installs.

**Key features**

- Compact D'Appolito two-way centre
- Dual 130mm bass/mid drivers and 26mm waveguide tweeter
- Dual front-firing ports for placement inside AV furniture

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** D'Appolito, 2-way
- **Drivers:** 2 x 130mm bass/mid + 26mm tweeter with waveguide centre plug
- **Ports:** Dual front-firing
- **Frequency response:** 45Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 20-160 Watts RMS recommended
- **Sensitivity:** 89dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms (minimum 4.3 Ohms)
- **Dimensions:** 180mm H x 450mm W x 300mm D
- **Weight:** 10kg each
