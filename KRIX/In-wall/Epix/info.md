# Epix

**Slug:** epix
**Subtitle:** High-end 3-way in-wall delivering floorstanding-class realism
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** in-wall
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/epix

## Description
The Epix lets you enjoy the realism of high-end floorstanding or centre-channel sound in an in-wall format, fitting into a standard 90mm frame. A fully enclosed back box maintains acoustic integrity and stops rear sound radiation transferring to adjoining rooms.

It suits both home theatre rooms and multi-functional living spaces in stereo and surround systems, and is available in vertical or horizontal configurations.

**Key features**

- High-end three-way in-wall speaker in a standard 90mm frame
- Four 130mm bass drivers, 130mm midrange, 26mm ring radiator tweeter
- Fully enclosed back box; vertical or horizontal configurations

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** 3-way
- **Drivers:** 4 x 130mm coated paper bass + 130mm midrange + 26mm ring radiator tweeter
- **Frequency response:** Down to 40Hz (in room); tweeter to 40kHz
- **Power handling:** Amplifiers up to 200W into 4 Ohms
- **Sensitivity:** 90dB
- **Impedance:** 4 Ohms
- **Fits:** Standard 90mm frame
