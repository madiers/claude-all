## Description
The IW-50 is a D'Appolito-configuration in-wall speaker often installed in Left-Centre-Right setups. Its fully enclosed rear cabinet guarantees consistent bass performance independent of the wall cavity and minimises sound transference into adjoining rooms.

The stainless-steel single-piece trimless grille is rust-resistant and supplied in a paintable white finish.

**Key features**

- D'Appolito in-wall speaker ideal for LCR setups
- Fully enclosed rear cabinet for consistent bass
- Rust-resistant paintable trimless stainless-steel grille

## Specifications

- **Configuration:** D'Appolito, 2-way; bass reflex, front-vented
- **Drivers:** Dual 100mm bass drivers + 26mm ring radiator tweeter
- **Frequency range:** 55Hz - 40kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 20-140 Watts RMS
- **Sensitivity:** 89dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** Nominally 6 Ohms (minimum 3.5 Ohms)
- **Fits:** Standard 70mm stud wall
