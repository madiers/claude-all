## Description
The Phonix In-wall delivers surround effects with clarity and power in a neat in-wall package, with low-frequency extension down to 45Hz. It is well suited to 5.1, 7.1, or immersive sound formats, and can be fixed to plastered studwork or cabinetry via its faceplate, with an optional black cloth grille for a concealed appearance.

The crossover uses close-tolerance, professional-grade components.

**Key features**

- In-wall surround with low-frequency extension to 45Hz
- 165mm laminated polypropylene cone and 26mm doped fabric dome
- Faceplate mounting with optional black cloth grille

## Specifications

- **Drivers:** 165mm laminated polypropylene cone, 33mm voice coil + 26mm doped fabric dome, ferro-fluid cooled
- **Low-frequency extension:** To 45Hz
- **Dimensions:** 388mm H x 260mm W x 120mm D (cabinet); 453mm x 325mm faceplate
