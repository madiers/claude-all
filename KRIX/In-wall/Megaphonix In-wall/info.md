## Description
The Megaphonix In-wall is designed for in-wall use in dedicated home cinemas, using high power-handling, high-sensitivity drivers to deliver cinema-grade impact and dynamics without a big-wattage amplifier. It can be mounted behind an acoustically transparent screen (without grille) or used with a grille in full view, and is braced and critically damped to improve bass extension.

It is part of the five-speaker Megaphonix family.

**Key features**

- High-output 10-inch in-wall cinema speaker
- Mountable behind an acoustically transparent screen
- Braced, critically damped; gold-plated binding posts

## Specifications

- **Drivers:** 255mm (10-inch) paper cone, 50mm voice coil + 26mm doped fabric dome with proprietary waveguide
- **Dispersion:** 90 degrees horizontal / 40 degrees vertical
- **Frequency range:** 40Hz - 20kHz (in room)
- **Power handling:** 50-250 Watts RMS
- **Sensitivity:** 95dB at 2.83V/1m
- **Impedance:** 8 Ohms
- **Crossover point:** 1.8kHz
- **Weight:** 23kg each
