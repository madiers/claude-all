# IW-30

**Slug:** iw-30
**Subtitle:** Compact in-wall for stereo music or home-theatre fronts/surrounds
**Brand:** krix
**Category:** in-wall
**Source:** https://www.krix.com.au/product/iw-30

## Description
The IW-30 is a compact in-wall speaker well suited to small-to-medium home theatre rooms as main or surround speakers, or as a left/right pair for stereo music. It fits within a 70mm wall cavity and has a fully enclosed back box that ensures acoustic integrity and prevents rear sound radiation from transferring to adjoining rooms.

A single-piece trimless grille is held by magnets and can be painted to match the room.

**Key features**

- Compact in-wall fitting a 70mm wall cavity
- 100mm doped paper cone bass driver and 20mm ring radiator tweeter
- Fully enclosed back box; paintable trimless magnetic grille

## Specifications

- **Drivers:** 100mm (4-inch) doped paper cone, 25mm voice coil + 20mm ring radiator tweeter in waveguide
- **Frequency range:** 65Hz - 40kHz
- **Fits:** 70mm wall cavity
- **Warranty:** 5 years
